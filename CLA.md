# Emend Contributor Licence Agreement

> ## ⚠️ Not legal advice, and not yet reviewed by a lawyer
>
> This is a starting point, modelled on the structure of widely-used contributor
> agreements such as the Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement. It has
> **not** been reviewed by a lawyer.
>
> Have counsel review it before making the repository public or accepting an
> outside contribution. A defective agreement is worse than an obvious absence of
> one, because it produces confidence in a chain of title that does not hold — and
> unlike most mistakes in this repository, it cannot be fixed by editing a file
> afterwards.

---

By contributing to Emend you agree to the following, for every contribution you
submit.

**1. Definitions.** "You" means the individual or legal entity making the
contribution. "Contribution" means any work of authorship you intentionally
submit to this project, in any form and through any channel — including code,
documentation, and issue or pull-request content.

**2. Copyright licence.** You grant the project owner a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare
derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and
distribute your Contribution and such derivative works.

*This is the clause dual licensing depends on.* "Sublicense" is what permits the
project owner to offer the Contribution under the AGPL and under a separate
commercial licence. Without it, a contribution can only ever be distributed under
the AGPL.

**3. Patent licence.** You grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence to make, use, offer to sell, sell,
import and otherwise transfer your Contribution, covering only those patent
claims you can license that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone
or by its combination with the project.

**4. You have the right to grant this.** You confirm that each Contribution is
your original work, or that you have the necessary rights to submit it. If your
employer has rights to work you create, you confirm either that you have their
permission, or that they have waived those rights for this project.

**5. Third-party material.** If a Contribution includes work that is not yours
and not covered by clause 4, you will identify it, together with its licence and
any restrictions, at the point of submission.

**6. No warranty.** Contributions are provided as-is, without warranty of any
kind, except as stated in clause 4.

**7. You keep your copyright.** This agreement licenses your Contribution to the
project owner; it does not assign it. You remain free to use your own work
however you wish.

## How to sign

Until an automated flow is in place, add your details below in the pull request
that carries your first contribution:

| Name | GitHub | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | | |

For anything beyond the first few contributors, use an automated check — the
common options attach a bot to pull requests and record signatures outside the
repository. That is worth setting up before the project attracts contributors,
not after.
